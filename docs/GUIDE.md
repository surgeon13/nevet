# The Nevet player guide

Welcome! Nevet is your garden's game. Every time you water, plant, pick or
feed the worms in the real garden, you tell Nevet, and your hero grows
stronger. Everyone's work adds up, and you can watch all the heroes play
together on a little 3D farm.

You don't need to know anything about computers. If you can open a web
page on a phone, you can play. Younger players: there's a
[guide just for you](KIDS.md).

## Joining

1. **Scan the QR code** on the screen by the garden with your phone's
   camera, or open the address someone gave you.
2. **Pick a hero name and a password.** The name is what other players
   see. The password is only for you: use one you don't use anywhere else,
   and don't tell it to anyone.
3. **That's it.** You stay logged in on that phone for 30 days.

Sometimes you need an **invite code** to join (the person who runs the
garden can give you one, and the QR code fills it in for you).

Played Nevet before under a name like `M`? Sign up with that same name and
you get your old hero back, with all its plants and history.

**Forgot your password?** Ask the person who looks after Nevet (the
admin). They can set a new one for you. You can change it yourself any
time on the **Account** page, from your hero page. If a password is typed
wrong 8 times, logging in pauses for a few minutes, so take a breath and
try again later.

## The dashboard: your home page

The first page you see. In the middle is the **brain**: fine threads of
mushroom roots (mycelium) reaching out to every part of Nevet, with little
sparks running along them. Each round button on a thread opens a section:
Plants, Heroes, Map, Events, Tags and more. The **Nevet** logo at the top
always brings you back here.

**Today at the farm** sits at the top. It shows what people logged today
(like "Dror watered the whole garden · 05:41") and what needs doing:

- plants nobody has watered for 3 days or more,
- worm bins nobody has fed for 7 days or more,
- plants that are ripe and ready to pick,
- seeds sown 14 days ago or more with no sprouts yet,
- family connections waiting for you to say yes,
- an event happening today, tomorrow or right now.

Under it, **Coming up** shows the next few events as cards you can swipe
through, each with Going / Maybe buttons and a QR code to invite people.

Every to-do has a button that takes you straight to logging it, with the
right plants already picked. The same summary pops up the first time you
open Nevet each day, when there's something to tell.

The tabs at the top: **Dashboard**, **Time** (the farm's story) and **XP**
(points and levels). The ⓘ button opens **About Nevet**: the garden's
numbers, the version and what's new.

Tapping the brain takes you to **the 3D farm**.

## Your hero

Every player is a hero. Open **Heroes**, then your hero, then customize.
A live 3D preview shows every change.

You can choose (all of it is optional, change it whenever you like):

- **How they look**: gender (female, male, non-binary, other, or leave it
  unset), 11 skin tones, 27 hairstyles (ponytail, braid, afro, locs,
  dreadlocks, punk spikes, and styles from history like a 1700s powdered
  wig, a samurai top knot, 40s victory rolls, a 60s beehive or 90s
  curtains), 16 hair colours, 13 eye styles (glasses, shades, a monocle,
  starry eyes...), 13 mouths and 9 kinds of facial hair (up to a wizard
  beard).
- **What they wear**: 20 outfits in 14 colours (tuxedo, disco, astronaut,
  superhero, royal robe...), 24 hats and head items (chef hat, top hat,
  propeller cap, viking helmet, flowerpot, halo...) and 6 kinds of earrings.
- **What they carry**: 19 tools (from a watering can to a rubber chicken,
  a frying pan, a bubble wand or a magic wand) and 15 things on their back
  (jet pack, balloons, a turtle shell, angel or dragon wings...).
- **A companion** (the Companion tab): a worm, snail, bee, sprout spirit,
  frog, chick, ladybug or hedgehog. Give it a name, a colour, a size and
  an accessory (bow, party hat, flower, top hat, crown).

Heroes are alive: they breathe, blink, and every few seconds wave, hop,
twirl or cheer. Tap your hero to make it do something; trying on a new
item makes it hop. (With "reduce motion" on in your phone's settings,
they only breathe and blink.)

**Deleting your hero:** on your hero page, open **Delete my hero** and
type your password. The hero goes to the **Recycle bin**, where the admin
can bring it back with everything it had. If it's deleted for good, the
garden keeps the history of what was done, just without the name.

## Logging what you did

This is the heart of Nevet. After you do something in the garden, open
**Log an action**, pick what you did (watering, planting, harvest,
weeding, pruning, feeding worms, check-up, sprouted, gave away...) and
which plants, and save. For a harvest you can add how much you picked. If you
watered everything, "the whole garden" counts for every plant.

Each action earns XP for your hero and shows up for everyone in Today at
the farm. Tags make this even faster: one tap on a sticker
([see Tags](#tags-tap-a-sticker-to-log)).

## Plants

**Plants** lists everything growing, with a little 3D picture next to each
one and a 3D garden on top.

**Adding a plant:** tap **Add a plant**, type its name and, if you know
it, the variety. Nevet works out what it is from the name, in English or
Hebrew ("Cherry tomato", "עגבניית שרי", or "Tomato" with variety
"Cherry"), and the preview shows what it will grow into. Wrong guess?
Pick the right one from the list.

**Adding lots at once:** **Plants → + Add many**, then type a list, one
plant per line:

```
olive x 5
mint x 3
yellow cherry x 3
basil x 3
```

"olive x 5", "5 olive", "olive 5" and "olive: 5" all work. You'll see a
preview before anything is saved. Each plant gets its own name (Olive 1 …
Olive 5) and its own history. Choose how they start (seeds, seedlings or
already growing) and, if the garden map has beds, which bed they go in.
Up to 200 at a time.

**The 3D models:** every plant looks like what it really is, at the stage
it's at: a seed mound, a sprout, a seedling, growing with flowers and green
fruit, ripe, harvested, and dried up at the end. There are about 90 kinds:
tomatoes, peppers, herbs, lettuces, root vegetables, squash, beans, corn,
sunflowers, berries, fruit trees, a date palm, mushrooms, microgreens, and
even compost worms and a beehive. On a plant's page you can turn the model
with your finger and tap each stage to see it from seed to harvest. If the
model is wrong, tap **change** under it.

## The garden map

**Map** shows the garden on a real map (streets or satellite photos),
close enough to draw single beds.

- **Find the garden:** search for the address, or tap the location
  button. The admin saves the spot so the map always opens there.
- **Add to the map:** draw a **bed or area** by tapping its corners, a
  **line** (paths, drip lines, fences), drop a **point** (a tap, compost,
  tree, shed, beehive, rain barrel, gate...) or **pin a plant or worm bin**
  where it grows.
- **Change things:** tap anything to name it, change its type or colour,
  add notes, drag its corners, move it or delete it. Deleted by mistake?
  Tap **Undo**, or find it later under **Recently removed** in the Garden
  list.
- Areas show their size in square metres, lines their length.
- **Garden** lists everything, with switches to show or hide each kind,
  and a button to download the map.

Everyone can add to the map, and it remembers who added what. The map
pictures come from the internet, so the phone needs a connection. "My
location" only works over a secure (https) address.

## The 3D farm

Tap the brain on the dashboard to visit the farm: every hero walks in
through the gate (up to 16, the most active first) and they do things
together. **Nothing on the farm changes the garden's real records**: it's
just for fun.

**How to look around:** drag to turn, pinch to zoom, tap a hero to follow
them (they wave at you), tap the ground to let go. The camera button saves
a picture. On a keyboard: keys 1-7 pick an activity, A turns auto on and
off, Esc goes back.

**What the heroes do** (Auto changes it about every minute):

- **Harvest festival**: picking ripe produce and filling the cart.
- **Watering round**: watering cans out, refilled at the pump. Plants
  that really haven't been watered for 3 days droop and get a water drop,
  and heroes go to them first.
- **Planting day**: sowing the new field and watching it sprout.
- **Campfire dance**: night falls and everyone dances around the fire.
- **Animal care**: feeding the worms, the chickens (they come running) and
  the ducks, and checking the beehives.
- **Play park** ⚽: football in two teams (the Tomatoes and the
  Blueberries) with a scoreboard, plus swings, a slide, a seesaw, a
  trampoline, a sandbox where a sandcastle grows, and kites overhead.
  There's also **the ring** 🥊: three heroes climb in, bow, and spar in a
  silly, friendly way. Every round is different: **giant gloves**, puffy
  **sumo suits**, bamboo **kendo swords** with helmets, or **mud
  wrestling** (they get muddier and muddier). Hits pop up as "POW!",
  "BOING!" or "SPLAT!", a big one knocks a hero flat with stars round their
  head (they hop right back up), and after the round the one with the
  most points wins and everyone bows. The board by the ring shows the
  round and the score, and other heroes come over to cheer.
- **Party!**: a stage with a DJ (whoever opened the farm), a light-up
  dance floor, lasers, a disco ball and confetti. Everyone does the same
  dance, from Disco and the Robot to the Chicken dance, the Hora, Debka
  and the Conga line, and a spotlight picks someone for a solo.

**The party synth:** the synth button opens a little music machine, and
the party dances to it. **303** is the bass: tap a step to turn it on (tap
again for accent, slide, rest) and drag up or down to change the note.
**909** is the drums: tap squares to place kicks, snares, claps and hats.
− and + change the speed, 🎲 makes a surprise pattern, and a tip line
explains whatever you touch. The speaker button starts and stops the
sound. Your pattern is remembered on your device.

**Your real garden on the farm:** once beds are drawn on the garden map,
the farm builds them in their real shapes, with each plant where it was
pinned, plus the worm beds, compost, greenhouse, pond, trees, taps,
beehives and more. A chalkboard in the yard shows this week's real
numbers. With no map yet, the farm uses four beds of its own.

**Nine lands around the farm:** the globe button picks the scenery, for
everyone:

- **Green valley**: meadows, apple trees and rolling hills.
- **Desert oasis**: dunes, date palms, cacti, camels and tumbleweeds.
- **Seaside**: a beach, a pier, a lighthouse, sailboats and gulls.
- **Deep forest**: tall pines, ferns, toadstools and deer.
- **Snowy mountains**: snowmen, a log cabin and falling snow.
- **Jungle**: giant trees, a waterfall, an old temple and parrots.
- **African bushland**: acacias, baobabs, giraffes, elephants and zebras.
- **Island lagoon**: palms, a turquoise lagoon, dolphins and a volcano.
- **City farm**: streets, cars, buildings lighting up at night and
  pigeons.

**Live channel:** the TV button turns the farm into a channel to leave on
a TV or tablet. No buttons, just a LIVE badge and a clock. The activities
follow the time of day (party and campfire at night), the camera glides
around, and when someone logs an action it pops up on screen ("Dana
harvested Cherry tomato") and the heroes act it out. Tap the screen to get
the exit button.

## Events: parties, festivals and gatherings

**Events** is where the farm plans its get-togethers: parties, festivals,
harvest days, workshops, volunteer days, markets, kids' days and campfire
nights.

- **Plan one:** give it a name, a kind, a day and time, a place, a
  description, how many people fit, and what the heroes on the 3D farm do
  meanwhile. Whoever made it (and the admin) can change, cancel or delete
  it.
- **Draw it on the map:** each event has its own map on top of the garden:
  the stage, dance floor, food, market stalls, camping, parking, the kids'
  area, paths, the emergency route, and pins for the entrance, toilets,
  drinking water, first aid, the meeting point and more. Sizes are measured
  as you draw.
- **Say if you're coming:** Going, Maybe or Can't come, how many guests
  you're bringing, and what you'll bring along. The quickest way is the
  **Coming up** cards on the dashboard: tap 🙌 Going or 🤔 Maybe right
  there (tap it again to take it back).
- **Invite people with a QR code:** every event has its own QR code (the
  little square button on its dashboard card, or **Invite people** on the
  event page). Show it on your phone, share the link, or print a
  **poster** for the gate. Whoever scans it lands on the event: players
  say they're coming in one tap, and new people make a hero on the way
  and go straight to the event. The event page counts the scans and the
  new heroes.
- **Help out:** the organizer lists jobs (setting up, sound, cooking, the
  gate, cleaning up) and you sign up for one.
- **On the day:** tap "I'm here" when you arrive. The gate has a big +/−
  counter for people walking in, and a live log of what's going on.
- **Afterwards:** give 1 to 5 stars and a comment, and see the farm
  camera's photos from those hours.
- **The numbers:** how many said they'd come and how many came, the
  busiest moment, a chart of people over time, and downloads for
  spreadsheets.

While an event is on, the 3D farm joins in with the event's name and
activity.

## Tags: tap a sticker to log

Tags are little stickers, cards or QR labels you put around the garden.
Tap one with your phone (or scan its QR code) and Nevet opens the right
page, already logged in as you.

- **Plant tag**: on a plant, bed, tree or worm bin. A tap shows big
  buttons: watered, checked, weeded, fed (for worms). One tap and it's
  logged.
- **Farm card**: for a whole-farm job like "Morning watering". One tap
  logs it.
- **Event check-in**: at the gate. A tap checks you in to the event.
- **Hero fob**: a key fob with your name on it, for the gate reader. Hold
  it to the reader, then tap other tags within two minutes and they're all
  logged as you.

To make tags, open **Tags** on the dashboard. You can print QR labels
(Tags → Print QR labels) on paper, or write the tag's link onto NFC
stickers (NTAG213 or NTAG215). The person who looks after the station can
help with stickers and fobs ([station guide](STATION.md#tags-and-the-flipper-zero)).

Only use tags and readers on stickers and fobs that belong to you or your
garden.

## Family and groups

Nevet is made for small groups: couples, families with children,
households, community gardens and volunteer teams.

- **Family & connections** (on every hero page): say who someone is to you
  (parent, child, partner, sibling, grandparent, housemate, friend,
  neighbour, mentor, fellow volunteer...). **Both people have to say yes.**
  Until then it shows as "waiting", and only the two of you (and the admin)
  can see it. Either of you can remove it any time.
- **Groups** (Heroes → Groups): a household, family, couple, community
  garden or volunteer team. Whoever creates it is the coordinator, who can
  add and remove people. Anyone can leave. Community gardens and volunteer
  teams are open for anyone to join.

Connections are just information. Nobody gets control over anyone else's
hero.

## Our farm story (Time)

The **Time** tab tells the farm's story in pink and green: two vines, green
for the garden (dark at the start, brighter towards today) and pink for its
people, winding around each other from the very first day
until today.

- Every day something happened is a **blossom**; busier days get bigger
  ones.
- Big moments get a glowing **heart**: the first day, new heroes joining,
  two heroes connecting, new groups, first plantings, first harvests, big
  harvests and events.
- A card tells each day's story: what happened, who was there, how much
  was picked, and the photos.
- Drag, scroll or use the slider to travel; tap a blossom to jump to it;
  ▶ plays the whole story.

## XP and levels

The **XP** tab shows points and levels. Your hero earns XP from real
garden work: the more you log, the higher your level.

## Small questions

**Does it cost anything?** No.

**Who can see my stuff?** Only people logged in to your garden's Nevet.
Your password is stored scrambled, so nobody can read it, not even the
admin. Family connections stay private until both people say yes.

**Can I play on a computer?** Yes, any web browser works.

**The farm is slow on my phone.** It draws in 3D on your device. Close
other apps, or turn on "reduce motion".

**Something looks broken.** Tell the person who looks after Nevet. They
can check [Troubleshooting](TROUBLESHOOTING.md).
