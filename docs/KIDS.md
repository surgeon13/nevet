# Nevet for kids 🌱

Hello, gardener! Nevet is a game about your real garden.

When you help in the garden, like watering the plants or feeding the
worms, you tell Nevet. Then your hero gets points and grows up, and the
whole garden team can see what you did. 🎉

## Make your hero

1. Ask a grown-up to help you join. 🧑‍🌾
2. Pick a **hero name**. It can be a fun name, it doesn't have to be your
   real one.
3. Pick a **password** with your grown-up.

> 🔒 **Your password is a secret.** Don't tell it to friends, and never
> type it anywhere except Nevet. Only you and your grown-up should know it.

Now dress up your hero! Pick hair, clothes, a funny hat, a tool (a magic
wand? a rubber chicken?) and even wings or a jet pack. Then pick a
**pet friend**: a worm, snail, bee, frog, chick, ladybug, hedgehog or a
little sprout spirit. Give your pet a name!

## Tell Nevet what you did

After you help in the garden:

1. Open **Log an action**.
2. Tap what you did: 💧 watered, 🌱 planted, 🍅 picked, 🪱 fed the worms...
3. Tap which plants.
4. Tap save. Your hero gets points! ⭐

**Even faster:** some plants have a sticker or a square code (a QR code)
next to them. Hold the phone near the sticker, or point the camera at the
code, and tap the big button. Done!

## Your hero's powers 💪

Your hero has five powers: Strength, Stamina, Agility, Wisdom and Heart.
They grow when you really help in the garden: picking and planting make
your hero stronger, watering gives stamina, feeding the worms grows a big
heart 💚. You can't buy powers. You grow them!

## Visit the farm 🚜

Tap the big brain picture on the first page. You'll see a 3D farm where
all the heroes play together! They can:

- pick vegetables 🥕
- water the plants 💧
- feed the chickens, ducks and worms 🐔🦆🪱
- play football and go on the swings and the slide ⚽
- have silly matches in the arena round the campfire 🔥: giant gloves,
  puffy sumo suits, bamboo swords or a big muddy splash fight, and then
  everyone bows
- dance at a party with lights and music 🪩
- dance around a campfire at night 🔥

Tap a hero to follow them, and they'll wave at you! 👋

**Make music:** tap the synth button and make your own beat. Tap the
squares to add drums, and tap 🎲 for a surprise song. The heroes dance to
your music!

**Change the land:** tap the globe 🌍 to put the farm in the desert, by
the sea, in a snowy forest, a jungle, on an island or in the middle of a
city!

The farm is just for fun. Nothing you do there changes the real garden.

## Parties and events 🎈

When the garden has a party or a market day, you'll see it on the first
page under **Coming up**. Ask your grown-up if you're going, then tap
🙌 **Going**. When you get there, tap **I'm here**!

Want to invite a friend? Tap the little square code button and let them
point their phone camera at it. 📲

## Be a good garden friend 💚

- Ask a grown-up before you pick anything or use tools.
- Only log what you really did. That's fair for everyone.
- Wash your hands after gardening and before eating.
- Be kind to the worms, the bees and the other heroes. 🐝
- If something on the screen looks strange or worries you, show a
  grown-up.

Have fun growing! 🌻
