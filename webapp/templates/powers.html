How fighting powers work

Every hero has five powers, and they grow from one thing only: real work in the garden that you log on Nevet. What you do in the real garden matters far more than anything on the 3D farm. Nothing can be bought, the arena kit and gear are only for looks, and nobody (not even an admin) can set powers by hand. It's all in the open in webapp/games.py, so anyone can read it and suggest changes.

The formula

power = (10 + 4 × log₂(1 + work) + {{ g_.CLASS_BONUS }} for your class's power) × {{ g_.LEVEL_POWER }}level − 1 × ({{ g_.RHYTHM_LOW }} + {{ (g_.RHYTHM_HIGH - g_.RHYTHM_LOW) | round(1) }} × rhythm)

  • Work: each kind of work builds its own power. Your first actions count the most (1 action ≈ +4, 15 ≈ +16, 100 ≈ +27), so steady work beats grinding. Stamina also grows with every different day you log something; Heart with every event check-in and volunteer job.
  • Exponential levels: every level multiplies every power by {{ g_.LEVEL_POWER }}. Level up by logging what you do: {% for lv, k in levels %}level {{ lv }} ×{{ k }}{{ ', ' if not loop.last }}{% endfor %}.
  • Fractal rhythm: how steadily you show up (below). Every single day ×{{ g_.RHYTHM_HIGH }}, one burst and nothing since ×{{ g_.RHYTHM_LOW }}.

🌀 The fractal rhythm

A coastline looks just as wiggly from a plane as from a beach: Benoit Mandelbrot measured patterns like that with a fractal dimension, how much of its space a pattern fills at every scale. Nevet does the same with the days you did real garden work (logged something or checked in to an event) over the last {{ g_.RHYTHM_DAYS }} days (or since you started, at least {{ g_.RHYTHM_MIN_DAYS }}): it covers them with boxes of 1, 2, 4 … {{ g_.RHYTHM_DAYS }} days, counts the boxes with a work day in them, and the slope of the counts on a log-log scale is your rhythm, from 0 to 1. Steady participation fills every scale, so it beats one big burst.

The Mandelbrot set and your fighting style

Your rhythm also puts your hero on the real axis of the Mandelbrot set, at c = {{ g_.MANDEL_WILD }} + {{ (g_.MANDEL_STEADY - g_.MANDEL_WILD) | round(2) }} × rhythm. In a bout, your form for each move is the next point of the orbit z → z² + c. Steady gardeners sit in the calm main cardioid, where the orbit stands still: the same strong form every move. Further left the orbit alternates (a strong move, then a weaker one), then splits into 4-, 8- and 16-move rhythms, and finally turns chaotic: brilliant one moment, awful the next. The swing never adds strength on average: it only makes you steady or wild.

In a bout

  • A move lands with the attacker's share of the two powers (each times their form that move): equals land half, twice as strong lands two in three, ten times as strong nine in ten.
  • Luck always plays a part: every move lands between {{ (g_.LUCK_FLOOR * 100) | int }}% and {{ ((1 - g_.LUCK_FLOOR) * 100) | int }}% of the time.
  • Spirit: a hero's first {{ g_.ROOKIE_MATCHES }} bouts count ×{{ g_.ROOKIE_K }}, and after {{ g_.COMEBACK_LOSSES }} losses in a row ×{{ g_.COMEBACK_K }}.
  • Mud wrestling uses every power a little: {% for k, w in g_.ARENA_STYLES.mud.uses.items() %}{{ k }} {{ (w * 100) | int }}%{{ ', ' if not loop.last }}{% endfor %}.
  • Everyone enters the campfire tournament. Football compresses powers and balances the teams, so it stays a game for everyone.
  • Glory (the arena's ranking) is just for fun: it never adds XP or powers. Only real garden work does.

chance a move lands = attacker ÷ (attacker + defender), kept between {{ (g_.LUCK_FLOOR * 100) | int }}% and {{ ((1 - g_.LUCK_FLOOR) * 100) | int }}%

So a hero who gardens every week and has reached level 5 wins about four bouts in five against a newcomer and a casual player. A newcomer who logs a few real actions in their first days already wins about one in six.
