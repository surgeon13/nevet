How powers work

Every hero has five powers. They grow from real work in the garden, the work you log on Nevet. Those who do, grow: nothing can be bought, gear and clothes are only for looks, and nobody (not even an admin) can set powers by hand. It's all in the open, in webapp/games.py, so anyone can read it and suggest changes.

Keeping matches fair

  • Strength is normalized: powers are compressed before a match (power 10 → 30, 20 → 48, 40 → 70, 70 → 90), so a veteran is stronger than a newcomer, not unbeatable.
  • Luck always plays a part: equals land half their moves; even the strongest hero never lands more than {{ (50 + g_.LUCK * 100) | int }}% against the weakest.
  • Spirit: a hero's first {{ g_.ROOKIE_MATCHES }} matches of a game get +{{ g_.ROOKIE_BOOST }}, and after {{ g_.COMEBACK_LOSSES }} losses in a row they get +{{ g_.COMEBACK_BOOST }}.
  • Everyone enters the campfire tournament, and football picks whoever played least in the last week first.
  • Balanced teams: football teams are picked in turns (A B B A …) from the strongest down, and the best keeper goes in goal.
  • Mud wrestling uses every power a little: {% for k, w in g_.ARENA_STYLES.mud.uses.items() %}{{ k }} {{ (w * 100) | int }}%{{ ', ' if not loop.last }}{% endfor %}. Football uses Strength and Wisdom to shoot, Wisdom and Agility to keep goal.
  • Glory (the arena's ranking) is just for fun: it never adds XP or powers. Only real garden work does.

chance a move lands = 50% + {{ (g_.LUCK * 100) | int }}% × tanh((attacker − defender) ÷ {{ g_.SPREAD }})

So a level-5 veteran wins about half of their bouts against two brand-new heroes, and each newcomer still wins about one in five.
